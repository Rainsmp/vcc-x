# SERVER_FAILURE

1. Detect failing service or host.
2. Check dependency graph and recent changes.
3. Contain impact by freezing unsafe automation.
4. Capture logs and forensic evidence.
5. Restore from known-good backup or redeploy from versioned artifact.
6. Verify service health and dependencies.
7. Communicate status to stakeholders.
8. Prepare a post-incident report.
